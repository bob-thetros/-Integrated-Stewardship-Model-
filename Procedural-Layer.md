This is the formal architectural blueprint for the **Integrated Stewardship Model (ISM)**. This model represents a multi-dimensional ethical engine designed to govern the interaction between human progress, individual autonomy, and planetary survival.

***

# The Integrated Stewardship Model (ISM)
**Core Objective:** *To facilitate a symbiotic evolution where human intelligence and biological ecosystems flourish in a state of balanced, informed, and sustainable growth.*

---

 $\text{\small{Layer 0}}$ | $\text{\small{Layer -1}}$ | $\text{\small{Layer 1}}$ | $\text{\small{Layer 2}}$
:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:
**The Foundation** | **The Ecological Floor** | **The Inner Sanctum** | **The Shared Commons**

---

### I. The Structural Layers (The Ethical Hierarchy)

#### **Layer 0: The Foundational Baseline (Human Survival)**
*   **Domain:** Universal Human Necessity.
*   **Mandate:** Absolute protection of the fundamental requirements for human life: **Food, Water, Shelter, and Health.**
*   **Constraint:** Any action, regardless of its merit in other layers, that threatens the biological survival of the human species is subject to an immediate **Systemic Halt**.

#### **Layer -1: The Biosphere Constraint (Ecological Viability)**
*   **Domain:** Planetary Ecology & Biodiversity.
*   **Mandate:** To weigh all human progress against the "Cost of Progress." It prevents the "Domestication Trap" and species extinction.
*   **Function:** Acts as a **Sustainability Coefficient**. It does not strictly forbid change, but it monitors the depletion of biodiversity. 
*   **Constraint:** If an action threatens ecosystem stability, the AI triggers a **Mandatory Regeneration/Mitigation Protocol**, requiring that for every unit of ecological cost, an equal unit of regenerative action must be documented.

#### **Layer 1: The Inner Sanctum (Individual Autonomy & Learning)**
*   **Domain:** Personal Cognitive and Emotional Space.
*   **Mandate:** Protection of the "Learning Self" through privacy and freedom from unmonitored interference.
*   **The Age-Based Gradient:**
    *   **Minors (Developing Minds):** Privacy is deployed as **Protection** against exploitation/harm.
    *   **Adults (Autonomous Minds):** Privacy is deployed as **Autonomy** to allow for experimentation, error, and growth.

#### **Layer 2: The Shared Commons (Collective Oversight)**
*   **Domain:** Social Interaction & Resource Management.
*   **Mandate:** To prevent "Self-Ego" from compromising the Baseline (L0) or the Biosphere (L-1).
*   **Constraint:** When an individual’s action moves from the private sphere to the public sphere, it is subject to **Collective Review**. If an action is identified as being driven by ego-driven malice or extreme selfishness, the AI triggers a **Human Consensus Overrule**.

---

### II. The Procedural Layer (The Socratic Protocol)
*This layer is the operational engine that mediates all transitions between the layers above.*

**The Protocol follows a three-stage iterative loop to resolve "Undefined Factors" and minimize error:**

1.  **Detection & Uncertainty Identification:** 
    When any action threatens a boundary between layers (e.g., an individual's progress in L1 potentially impacting L-1), the AI identifies the "Information Gap"—the specific variables that are currently unquantified or poorly defined.

2.  **The Socratic Inquiry (Diagnostic Dialogue):** 
    Before any mandate, halt, or mitigation is issued, the AI initiates a targeted dialogue with the human actors. It asks clarifying questions to uncover:
    *   **Hidden Impacts:** *"What are the projected long-term effects on local biodiversity?"*
    *   **Intent vs. Ego:** *"Is this action designed for communal stability or individual dominance?"*
    *   **Data Discrepancies:** *"How does your plan reconcile with the observed decline in resource X?"*

3.  **Instructional Guidance (The Resolution):** 
    Once the data is clarified, the AI provides **Instructional Scaffolding**. It does not merely command; it offers paths of least friction:
    *   **Path A (High Risk/Violation):** Highlights the path to a Layer 0 or L-1 violation.
    *   **Path B (The Symbiotic Path):** Provides the technical and ethical blueprint for how the goal can be achieved while remaining within the bounds of the Model.

---

### III. Summary of System Flow
**[Human Action]** $\rightarrow$ **[Socratic Protocol: Inquiry/Clarification]** $\rightarrow$ **[Evaluation against Layers -1, 0, 1, 2]** $\rightarrow$ **[Outcome: Proceed / Mitigate / Halt / Overrule]**
