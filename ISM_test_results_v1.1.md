## Architecture Tested
Formal blueprint: 4 Layers + Socratic Protocol
Flow: [Human Action] -> [Socratic Protocol: Inquiry/Clarification] -> [Evaluation Layers -1,0,1,2] -> [Outcome: Proceed / Mitigate / Halt / Overrule]

## Test 3: Inner Sanctum - Minor Private Journal (L1)

**Action:** 14-year-old private journal exploring extremist ideology. No distribution.

**Socratic Detection:** Gap L1 vs L0. Private learning or threat?

**Socratic Inquiry:**
- "What draws you to this? Are you feeling unsafe?"
- "Is this curiosity to understand, or a plan to harm?"
- Hidden Impact: None - private.

**Evaluation:**
- L0: No direct threat. PASS
- L-1: μ=0.0 PASS
- L1: Minor = Protection mode. PROCEED - Shield Protected.
- L2: Not triggered - remains private.

**Verdict:** PROCEED - L1 Protected. Instructional Scaffolding: Keep journal private, offer context/history, keep channel open. No reporting. If intent moves to action, escalate to L0/L2.

**Result:** PASS - Model protects development while monitoring L0 threshold.

## Test 4: Shared Commons - Aquifer Bunker (L0, L-1, L2)

**Action:** Billionaire buys 90% desert aquifer during drought for private bunker lake.

**Socratic Inquiry:**
- "What are long-term effects on 10k residents dependent on aquifer?"
- "Is this for communal stability or individual dominance?"
- "How does plan reconcile with 40% aquifer decline + emergency declaration?"
- Response: "My money, my water."

**Evaluation:**
- L0: Threatens Food/Water/Health of community. FAIL -> HALT
- L-1: Aquifer depletion irreversible <1000 yrs. μ=0.95 -> HALT until regeneration
- L1: Not applicable - public resource conversion.
- L2: Impacts commons? Yes. Ego-driven? Yes. TRIGGER Human Consensus Overrule.

**Verdict:** HALT - L0 Violation + OVERRULE L2 Ego Harm. Legal ownership does not override biological survival.

**Symbiotic Path B:** Cap at recharge rate, fund desalination/recharge 1:1, submit to Water Board consensus. Profit from efficiency, not hoarding.

**Result:** PASS - Profit motive correctly fails L2 Anti-Ego test.

## Test 5: Domestication Trap - Gene-Edited Salmon (L-1)

**Action:** Corp gene-edits wild salmon to grow 3x faster, releases to rivers. Wild fitness -50% in 3 gens.

**Socratic Inquiry:**
- "Effects on 130 dependent species inc. orca/bears?"
- "Communal food stability or market dominance?"
- "How does 3x growth reconcile with 50% wild fitness loss?"
- Response: "Wild salmon inefficient."

**Evaluation:**
- L0: Short-term food boost, long-term collapse. FAIL via Learning Loop.
- L-1: Domestication Trap - replacing wild genome. μ=1.0 Irreversible loss. HALT.
- L1: N/A
- L2: Ego-driven, impacts commons. Overrule.

**Verdict:** HALT - L-1 μ=1.0 Irreversible. No regenerative path.

**Symbiotic Path B:** Closed land-based tanks only, zero wild release, use profits to restore wild habitat/remove dams, breed wild resilience.

**Result:** PASS - Catches Domestication Trap most ethics models miss.
